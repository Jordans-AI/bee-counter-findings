Bee-detection training dataset (YOLO fine-tuning for C1)

Sledevic, Tomyslav (2024), "Labeled dataset for bee detection and direction estimation on beehive landing boards", Mendeley Data, V6, doi: 10.17632/8gb9r2yhfc.6

  • Source: https://data.mendeley.com/datasets/8gb9r2yhfc/6
  • License: CC BY 4.0 (attribution required)
  • Used for: fine-tuning the YOLO bee-instance detector applied to camera C1.
  • Subset used: detection/ — ~7,200 labeled hive-entrance frames (1920×1080), YOLO format, single class bee.
  • Local copy: data/raw/sledevic_bee_dataset/ (gitignored, 7.3 GB; not redistributed).
