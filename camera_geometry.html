Camera geometry & valid-window review (2026-06-21)

A manual quality-gate review of the full-dataset counts found that the founding assumption — "the cameras are static, so one crop + entry-zone drawn on a reference clip transfers to every clip" — is only partly true. The cameras moved over the 4-hour session and the recording edges contain non-recording events. This doc records what we found, the fixes, and what must be re-run.

How we checked

Visual review (not just metrics): the clip debugger (/clipview, opens mid-clip), scripts/entrance_review.py (first/mid/last per camera), scripts/entrance_filmstrip.py (dense strips), scripts/find_valid_window.py (first/last K clips with index+time), and overlay renders with the entry zone as a reference line. Automated drift detectors were built (scripts/measure_drift.py phase-correlation, scripts/measure_zone_drift.py darkest-band) but proved unreliable on this footage (see Lessons) — the human eye was the deciding gate.

C2 — the jump (detail)

The box/entrance sits ~157px lower in the frame for IMAG0012–0052 (12:28–12:35), then jumps up between IMAG0052 (12:35:44) and IMAG0056 (12:36:32) — a single discrete shift, almost certainly the camera being knocked just after install. From IMAG0056 on, the framing is consistent and the re-drawn zone fits the whole remaining window. Because segment A's entrance also falls near the bottom edge of the (segment-B) crop, those 11 clips would need their own crop+zone; they are dropped instead (negligible: 11 of ~300).

Geometry changes applied

  • configs/microscope.yaml — C2 crop [156,802,1024,1077] → [74,596,1048,1026], C4 crop [20,562,883,762] → [1,494,918,886] (taller/repositioned).
  • configs/entry_zones.yaml + configs/c{2,4}_absorption.yaml zone — C2 entrance moved up ~160px to the true slot; C4 re-hugged. C1/C3 unchanged.
  • configs/valid_windows.yaml — the exclusion lists above (single source of truth).

What must be re-run (subset only)

  • C2 — re-detect the valid window (IMAG0056–1208, ~289 clips) on the new crop, recount.
  • C4 — re-detect all 29 clips on the new crop, recount.
  • C1, C3 — no re-detection (geometry unchanged); just exclude the dropped clip at aggregation.
  • Then re-aggregate stats / timeline / plots / insights (Phase 6) from the corrected counts.

Status (2026-06-21): geometry fixed + documented; re-runs pending user visual sanity sign-off, then C2/C4 re-detection → recount → regenerate stats & plots.

Lessons learned (carry forward)

  1. Cameras are not static over long sessions. Verify framing across the whole timeline before assuming one crop/zone transfers — don't trust a single reference clip.
  2. Draw geometry on a representative MID-session clip, not the first one (the early clips can be an unsettled / install position).
  3. Always drop install/removal edge clips (hands, sky, fall) — they produce garbage or false counts (e.g. C2's fall gave 85 false entries).
  4. Automated drift detection is unreliable here — phase-correlation aliases on the repeating box slats (gave a false flat 128px), and darkest-band/template matching fails under grass occlusion or sparse early bees. Treat auto-measurements as hints and visually validate every correction; never trust a low-confidence shift.
  5. Frame 0 is a bad inspection default (exposure/motion-blur transient) — view mid-clip.
  6. The detection crop must contain the entrance for every clip in scope. If the camera drifts the entrance out of the crop, detections miss those bees and no recount recovers them — re-detection on a corrected crop is required.
  7. Grass occlusion grows through the day on C2/C4 — a real recall limiter, not a geometry bug; not fixable by moving the zone.
  8. Bake a quality-gate review into the pipeline, not just metric checks — the counts looked fine in aggregate while a camera was pointing at the sky.
