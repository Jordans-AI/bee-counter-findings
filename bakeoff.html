Key findings

  • No candidate clears PASS bar (0.85 mean / 0.70 min-clip).
  • High-scoring candidates with 0 total events are vacuous winners. A3, A1, B1, B2 emit 0 crossings on most clips. Their scores reflect matching the judge's 0/0 on quiet windows, not real counting ability. They cannot be improved by tuning — the pipeline is broken upstream.
  • The detect-→-track-→-cross pipeline fails at high bee density. B1 GroundingDINO confirmed detection works (15+ bees/frame in smoke tests), but ByteTrack ID switching across the dense crowd at frame_step=3 (10 fps) means no track persists long enough to register a confirmed line crossing. B2 OWLv2 same problem.
  • COCO-pretrained detectors (A1 YOLO11x, A3 RT-DETR) have no bee class. Confirmed: both emitted 0 bee-shaped detections at conf=0.05.
  • C1 MOG2 is the only method emitting real crossings. Mean 0.675, scoring 0.70 / 0.90 / 0.45 / 0.65 across cameras. Over-counts on C3 (4/11 vs 4/6) and zero-counts on C4 (0/0 vs 2/5).
  • C2 Farneback flow accumulator severely over-counts (729/464 on C1 vs 0/4). Threshold mis-calibrated; only C1/C2 scored before run truncated.
  • A2 YOLO11x+SAHI never ran (paired agent exited before reaching A2 stage).

Winner pick (for Phase 2 improvement)

Winner: C1 MOG2 — the only candidate actually counting crossings. Mean 0.675. Runner-up: B1 GroundingDINO — best detection signal (real bees found per frame), but counting pipeline broken.

Phase 2 plan: rather than narrowly "tune the winner", restructure the counter: 1. Tune C1 MOG2 per-camera to reduce over-count on C3 and recover events on C4 (raise area threshold, lower hysteresis, narrow band). 2. Build a hybrid B1+MOG2: use MOG2 motion gating to filter false positives, GroundingDINO to verify blob is a bee, full-rate (frame_step=1) line crossing. 3. If still below PASS, fall back to per-frame Opus-as-detector on the entrance band (Tier 3) — bypasses tracking entirely.
