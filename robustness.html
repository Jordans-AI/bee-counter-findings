3. What this says about overfitting

  • The shipped configs are centred, not knife-edge. Every camera's baseline ratio sits at 0.90–1.02 (the middle of the ±20% band), and the 1-off F1 range is narrow (≤0.17 wide on each camera). A config fit to exact GT frames would show a spike — a baseline far better than its neighbours; instead the neighbours bracket the baseline tightly. So the count band is a plateau the shipped pick sits near the centre of.
  • The count is robust to tightening and to most loosening, but a hard recall-loosening step overshoots. The in-band fraction is below 1 only because the most aggressive recall levers (min_len↓, gate_px↓ on the less dense cameras) push the ratio to ~1.25–1.31 — i.e. the failure mode is over-counting from fragmentation when association is loosened, exactly the behaviour decisions.md predicts. The shipped values back off one notch from that edge.
  • Temporal F1 is a tolerance artefact; the count is not. F1 climbs monotonically with tol (≈0.55→0.87 from 5f→30f on C1) while the ratio is identical at every tol. The project's headline — all four cameras in the ±20% count band — therefore does not depend on the arbitrary ±15f window; only the secondary F1 number does, and tol=15 is a conservative (low) choice.
